## 1. Purpose
The Parties want to discuss a possible collaboration: connecting the PrescribeX prescription-audit service to CMED's
prescription system and running a pilot with CMED doctors (the "Purpose").

## 2. Confidential information
"Confidential Information" means any non-public information one Party (the "Discloser") gives the other (the
"Recipient") for the Purpose, in any form, including: software, model weights, prompts, rule lists and evaluation
results; system designs, API specifications and data formats; business plans and prices; and any prescription or
patient-related data. Information is confidential if it is marked so, or if a reasonable person would understand it
to be confidential.

It does **not** include information that the Recipient can show: (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the
Recipient; (b) the Recipient already had lawfully, without a duty of confidence; (c) the Recipient received lawfully
from a third party without a duty of confidence; or (d) the Recipient developed independently without using the
Discloser's information.

## 3. Obligations
The Recipient will:
1. use Confidential Information only for the Purpose;
2. share it only with its employees, students, supervisors and advisers who need it for the Purpose and are bound by
   confidentiality duties at least as strict as these;
3. protect it with at least reasonable care, and no less care than it uses for its own similar information;
4. not copy, reverse-engineer or decompile software or models it receives, except as needed for the Purpose;
5. tell the Discloser promptly in writing of any unauthorised use or disclosure it learns of.

## 4. Patient data
No identifiable patient data will be exchanged under this Agreement. Any sharing of prescription data (even
de-identified) for development or the pilot needs a separate written data-sharing agreement that covers the data
fields, de-identification, storage place, retention, access and breach steps, and requires ethics approval where
applicable. Until then, sample data shared for format review must be synthetic or fully de-identified.

## 5. Required disclosure
If law or a court or regulator requires the Recipient to disclose Confidential Information, it may do so, but will
first (where lawful) tell the Discloser and disclose only what is required.

## 6. Publication
This Agreement does not stop the Project from publishing research results, provided no CMED Confidential Information
is disclosed. The Project will send CMED any draft that mentions CMED at least [30] days before submission, and will
remove CMED Confidential Information on request.

## 7. Ownership; no licence
All Confidential Information stays the property of the Discloser. Nothing in this Agreement grants a licence or any
other right, or obliges either Party to enter into any further agreement.

## 8. Return or destruction
On written request, or when discussions end, the Recipient will return or destroy the Discloser's Confidential
Information and confirm this in writing, except copies it must keep by law (which stay confidential).

## 9. Term
This Agreement lasts **[2] years** from the effective date. The duty of confidentiality continues for **[3] years**
after it ends (and indefinitely for patient data).

## 10. General
- No warranty: information is provided "as is".
- Governing law: the laws of Bangladesh; courts of Dhaka.
- Changes must be in writing and signed by both Parties.
- This is the whole agreement on its subject. It may be signed in counterparts and electronically.
